The rest of it

I came into IT the long way around, which is why I trust what I have built and tested over what a vendor slide claims. What I bring to a team is a combination that is rarer than it should be: someone who can design the architecture, explain it to the people paying for it, and then lead the work to ship it.

Most of what I know about telecom and cloud networking I learned being responsible for something that could not go down. The certifications came after the scar tissue.

Outside of work I am usually outside — the photo up top is from the summit of Mt Ellinor. I also keep a cooking blog, which is where the Cooking Pro recipes came from. Family first: my wife, my daughter, and whatever trail we have decided to drag ourselves up that weekend.
